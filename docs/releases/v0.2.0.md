Agents now share simulators and emulators and wait their turn, and a workspace can switch between iOS
and Android without losing its device.

## Highlights
- **Device sharing.** At the device limit, a workspace shares a running device of its kind and queues for
  it instead of waiting for a slot of its own. A device stays reserved between an agent's calls and frees
  after 90 s idle. Once others wait, the holder is warned and then gives the device up, never during a
  call. The in-use overlay shows the holder, the queue and a **Take over** button; the sidebar, overview
  cards, tab bar and status bar show the place in line.
- **Platform switch.** A workspace has one platform attached at a time. Switch from the device panel
  picker, or let the agent do it. The other platform's device is parked and reused when you switch back,
  with no new clone.
- **New MCP tools.** `device_acquire { wait_s, platform }` and `device_release`, plus a `sharing` block
  in `device_info`. A device tool that waits past its budget fails with its place in line and the holder.
- **Hardening.** Large files split by concern, a sans-IO device pool, views that do no I/O, PTY watchdog
  and drain fixes, and dead code removed.

## Known gaps
- Device sharing and platform switching are tested end to end through the MCP bridge but not yet walked
  through on screen.
- `HANGAR_DEVICE_PLATFORM`, `HANGAR_DEVICE_ID` and `ANDROID_SERIAL` keep naming the old device after a
  switch; `device_acquire` and `device_info` give the real one.
- Agents learn of a device grant by polling `device_acquire` with `wait_s`; there is no push.
